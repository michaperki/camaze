The short version

Spike detection compares yesterday's usage to your own trailing baseline. Budget alerts watch your month-end forecast, not just spend so far. Both email you immediately, separate from the daily digest.

Spike detection

Tuned to your own baseline, not a fixed number

Every day, camaze compares yesterday's usage to your trailing 7-day median — which mode applies depends on how much you'd normally spend on a given day.

Turn on alerts →
Ratio mode
When you have a baseline
3× median $1 floor

Fires when yesterday's usage is at least 3× your 7-day median and at least $1 — the floor keeps a 20¢-vs-5¢ day from counting as a spike just because the ratio looks dramatic.

Absolute mode
When a week is quiet
$1 wake threshold

When your median is near zero, a ratio against it is meaningless — so camaze instead fires on any usage at or above $1.

Budget alerts

A warning before the month closes, not after

Set a monthly budget once, and camaze watches your month-end forecast against it — not just spend so far. When the forecast crosses a threshold you choose (80% by default), you get an email. Budget and spike alerts are sent separately from each other and from the daily digest, so an urgent one never waits behind a routine one.
